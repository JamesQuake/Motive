Let us insure your commercial van

For commercial van operators, liability and physical damage coverage are essential as you move through traffic, delivery routes, job sites, and customer locations.

Motive helps van operators review how the vehicle is used so coverage matches real business exposure.

Independent contractor protection

If you are an independent contractor, you may fall under an employer or platform policy at certain times. That does not always mean your van is protected in every situation.

We help identify when additional coverage may be needed, especially when the vehicle is not under dispatch or is being used outside a narrow contract.

Coverage for service businesses

Commercial van insurance can support electricians, plumbers, couriers, cleaners, caterers, installers, and other service businesses that depend on vans to operate.

The right policy can help protect your vehicle, your business, and the income tied to staying on the road.
