Homeowners insurance generally addresses an owned residence and covered personal property. Renters insurance primarily protects eligible personal belongings and liability exposures without insuring the landlord's building.

Condo insurance typically focuses on the unit owner's responsibilities and property not covered by the condominium association's master policy. Motive can help customers review the appropriate coverage type for their living situation.

Liability-only auto insurance generally covers specified injury or damage claims involving other people or their property, subject to limits and conditions.

"Full coverage" is an informal term often used to describe a policy that also includes comprehensive and collision coverage. It does not mean every loss is insured. Motive can explain the actual coverages and exclusions so you know what you are purchasing.

A deductible is the amount you generally pay toward a covered claim before the applicable insurance payment begins.

Choosing a higher deductible may lower the premium, but it also increases what you may need to pay following a loss. Motive helps customers compare deductibles and coverage options based on their financial situation and insurance needs.

An annual review is useful, especially before renewal. You should also consider a review after buying a vehicle, moving, renovating a home, adding a driver, or making another significant change.
