Frequently Asked Questions About Motive Insurance

Motive helps customers review personal auto, home, renters, condo, motorcycle, commercial vehicle, trucking, general liability, workers' compensation, and specialty transportation insurance options.

We work with insurance carriers including Progressive, GEICO, and Liberty Mutual, subject to applicable appointments and product availability. Our purpose is to help customers understand their options, identify potential gaps, and select coverage suited to their needs.

Motive Insurance Solutions serves customers in Illinois, Indiana, and Wisconsin, subject to licensing, carrier availability, and the insurance product requested.

Our office locations include Matteson and Wood Dale, Illinois. Whether you need personal vehicle coverage, household protection, or commercial insurance, contact Motive to discuss the options available for your location.

For personal insurance, prepare your contact information, vehicle or property details, driver information, and current policy if available.

For commercial insurance, additional information may include your business operations, vehicles, drivers, employees, transportation activities, and contractual insurance requirements.

Motive uses these details to help identify eligible coverage options and provide a more meaningful comparison.

Motive helps customers review important differences between eligible insurance options, including liability limits, deductibles, policy features, exclusions, and available endorsements.

A lower price does not always mean equivalent protection. Before changing coverage, we can help you review important differences and coordinate requested effective dates so that you can avoid unintended gaps.

You can contact Motive Insurance Solutions through our website at www.motivetoday.com or call 888-943-0888.

Our team can assist with quote inquiries, policy questions, requested changes, Certificates of Insurance, and guidance on contacting the appropriate insurer for claims.
