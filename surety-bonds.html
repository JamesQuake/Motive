Surety Bonds and Trusts that gets it right

Bonds and trusts

BMC-84 bonds, often known as BMC-84 bonds, are surety bonds required by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration to help protect shippers and carriers from non-payment or fraudulent activity.

A BMC-85 trust can also satisfy financial responsibility requirements by placing funds into a dedicated trust account.

The role of trust in transportation compliance

A BMC-85 trust generally ties up capital that could otherwise support marketing, technology, hiring, or growth. For many smaller companies, that liquidity tradeoff matters.

A surety bond may provide a different path to compliance, with premium costs affected by creditworthiness and other risk factors.

Choosing the right provider

Bond and trust providers should be financially sound, responsive, and experienced with transportation claims and compliance.

BMC-84 & Freight Broker Bond Questions

A BMC-84 is a surety bond used to satisfy federal financial security requirements for certain freight brokers and freight forwarders.

Freight brokers and applicable freight forwarders generally must maintain $75,000 in required financial security. Motive can help customers review their bond needs and available assistance while directing regulatory questions to the appropriate authority.

A BMC-84 is a surety bond issued by a qualified surety provider. A BMC-85 is a trust fund arrangement established through an eligible financial institution.

Both are methods of satisfying the applicable $75,000 federal financial security requirement, but their application processes, financial commitments, and obligations differ. Motive can help customers understand the distinction before choosing an appropriate provider.

With a BMC-84 surety bond, an approved applicant generally pays a bond premium rather than depositing the full $75,000 face amount. Premiums vary with underwriting and the applicant's financial profile.

A BMC-85 trust requires qualifying financial assets or security under its terms. Motive can help explain these differences so customers understand the potential upfront financial commitment.

A new brokerage may qualify for a BMC-84 surety bond, although approval, premium, and other terms depend on underwriting.

Financial history, credit factors, and business details may be considered. Motive can help new freight brokers understand the application information they may need and explore available bond assistance.
