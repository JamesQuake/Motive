Know what to prepare

Your truck has a job to do. Start your quote with the details that describe it.

Prepare your vehicle, cargo, operating area, and driver information, then request a quote through Motive’s Trucking Insurance form.

Whether you are arranging coverage or comparing options, accurate information gives the conversation a useful starting point.

The quick answer

What do you need for a box truck insurance quote?

Motive’s trucking form asks about your business, where the truck is kept, the loads you carry, how far you drive, your vehicle, and your coverage needs.

Gather those details before starting. Check identifying numbers and addresses carefully, and contact Motive if you are unsure how a question applies.

01 / Get ready

Prepare your information before you start

  1. Contact and business information
  2. Garaging location
  3. Cargo and operating radius
  4. Vehicle details
  5. Driver information
  6. Coverage information
01

Contact and business information

Have your personal and business details available, along with a phone number and email where Motive can reach you. Review your entries before submitting.

02

Garaging location

Use the location where the vehicle is actually kept. The form requests the parking ZIP code and garaging address.

03

Cargo and operating radius

Describe the loads you transport and select the radius that reflects your operation. If the available choices do not clearly fit your work, speak with Motive before guessing.

04

Vehicle details

Have your truck’s VIN available and review the vehicle-value question. Make sure the information describes the truck you want insured.

05

Driver information

Prepare your driver’s license information and complete the applicable driver questions. Include CDL details where requested and relevant.

06

Coverage information

Review the coverage selections, cargo entries, current-insurance question, and other applicable items in the form.

Submitting your preferences starts a request. It does not establish that insurance is in place.

02 / Know the differences

Understand what you are comparing

A box truck insurance conversation can involve different types of protection. Confirm which coverages are included rather than assuming one selection protects the entire operation.

Look beyond the number

Compare more than the payment

When reviewing quotes, compare the operation described, coverage limits, deductibles, exclusions, and payment terms.

A lower payment deserves a closer look if the quotes use different vehicle details or provide different protection. Ask an agent to explain material differences before making your decision.

A little clarity

Box truck insurance quote questions

Which form should I use for a box truck?

Use Motive’s Trucking Insurance form and complete the questions that describe your vehicle and operation.

Why does the form ask what I haul?

Your cargo description helps explain the work being quoted. Use an accurate description of the loads you transport.

Which operating radius should I choose?

Select the available option that reflects your work. Contact Motive if you are unsure which option applies.

Does vehicle coverage automatically protect the freight?

Do not assume it does. Vehicle physical damage and motor cargo address different exposures. Review the applicable coverage with your agent.

What if I need help completing the form?

Call 888-943-0888 for assistance with your request.

Does submitting the form start coverage?

No. A quote request does not bind insurance or change an existing policy. Confirm coverage and its effective date with your agent.
