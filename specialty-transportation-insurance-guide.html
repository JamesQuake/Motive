What Determines Your Transportation Insurance Needs?

Insurance companies review the operation, not just the vehicle.

Vehicles

The number, type, age and value of your vehicles affect coverage and pricing.

Drivers

Driving history, experience, qualifications and driver numbers affect underwriting.

Passengers or Property

Transporting people creates different risks than moving vehicles, equipment or customer property.

Operating Area

Routes, operating radius, service territory and interstate work can influence requirements.

Contracts

Customers may require specific liability limits, documentation or endorsements.

Business Growth

Additional vehicles, drivers, locations or services can change existing coverage needs.

Before You Accept a Transportation Contract

A contract can require more than proof of insurance. Before signing, compare the requested coverage with your existing policies.

  • Liability limits
  • Passenger liability
  • Additional insured endorsements
  • Certificates of Insurance
  • Workers’ compensation
  • Commercial auto limits
  • Cargo or property coverage
  • Vehicle-specific requirements

Review the requirements before work begins so potential gaps can be identified early.

Specialty Transportation Insurance Questions

Is specialty transportation insurance different from regular commercial auto?

Yes. Passenger transportation, customer vehicles, medical transportation, towing and contracts can require a broader review than standard commercial auto.

Does every transportation company need passenger liability?

Not always. Passenger liability concerns operations that carry people. Towing, vehicle transport and delivery businesses may have different priorities.

Can Motive review insurance requirements in a contract?

Yes. Motive can help review required limits, certificates, endorsements and other insurance terms before coverage decisions are made.

What information should I have ready for a quote?

Prepare your business, vehicle and driver details, service area, passengers or property transported, contracts, claims history and current insurance.

Can my insurance change as my transportation business grows?

Yes. More vehicles, drivers, services or operating areas can change your exposures and coverage options.
