What Is a BMC-34 Filing?

A BMC-34 is proof of cargo liability insurance filed with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration for qualifying household goods motor carriers.

  1. 01 / THE COVERAGE

    Cargo Policy

    The actual insurance coverage for covered household goods cargo losses.

  2. 02 / THE PROOF

    BMC-34

    Proof of qualifying cargo coverage, submitted by the insurer or authorized electronic filer.

  3. 03 / THE RECORD

    FMCSA

    The filing becomes part of the carrier’s federal insurance and operating authority record.

A BMC-34 is not a separate insurance policy. It is the filing that shows the required cargo liability insurance is in place. Motive can help coordinate the insurance side of that process.

Household Goods Insurance & BMC-34 FAQs

What is a BMC-34 filing?

A BMC-34 is proof of cargo liability insurance filed with FMCSA for qualifying household goods motor carriers. It connects the required cargo coverage to the carrier’s federal insurance record.

Who needs a BMC-34 filing?

It is relevant to household goods motor carriers operating under applicable interstate authority. The actual operation and authority determine the filing requirements; a furniture or appliance delivery business is not automatically classified as a household goods carrier.

Is BMC-34 the same as cargo insurance?

No. The cargo policy provides the coverage, subject to its terms. The BMC-34 is the filing that provides proof of that qualifying coverage to FMCSA.

Who files the BMC-34 with FMCSA?

The insurance company or its authorized electronic filer submits the filing. Motive can help coordinate the insurance side of the process.

What insurance does an interstate moving company need?

An interstate moving company may need commercial auto and public liability, motor truck cargo, general liability, workers’ compensation, physical damage, umbrella or excess liability, and property or storage coverage. Required filings and the appropriate coverage depend on the operation.

What is the difference between BMC-34 and a public liability filing?

BMC-34 provides proof of cargo liability insurance. A public liability filing addresses bodily injury and property damage financial responsibility for commercial vehicle operations. A qualifying carrier may need both.
