Specialty Transportation Insurance Questions

Specialty transportation includes operations such as non-emergency medical transportation, wheelchair transportation, towing, roadside assistance, passenger shuttle services, and vehicle transportation.

Each business carries different risks involving passengers, vehicles, property, or equipment. Motive helps evaluate those operational differences so customers can review appropriate insurance options rather than relying on a generic commercial auto quote.

NEMT businesses should review commercial auto liability, passenger exposures, general liability, and potentially other coverage related to their services.

Vehicle accessibility equipment, passenger assistance, driver qualifications, and transportation contracts may affect coverage requirements. Motive can help operators review these details when exploring insurance options for eligible transportation businesses.

Towing businesses should review commercial auto coverage and protection for customers' vehicles while being towed or held in the business's care.

On-hook towing and garagekeepers coverage are examples of specialized protections that may be relevant. Motive can help operators understand the differences and review available coverage options based on how the business actually operates.

Transporting passengers introduces risks and insurance requirements that differ from transporting merchandise or equipment.

Passenger capacity, vehicle configuration, transportation contracts, and applicable regulations can influence the type and amount of coverage required. Motive helps customers identify these differences and review insurance options suited to their particular operations.
